# 🚗 BMW Dashboard Sales Analytics

##  About The Project
An end-to-end Data Analytics project analyzing **BMW** global sales performance across multiple vehicle categories and regions (2022–2024). This project was developed as part of the data analysis training at **SkillEra Academy**, under the supervision and guidance of Eng. **Mohamed Nasr**.

The suite contains **6 interactive dashboards**, offering deep operational insights into total revenue, volume sales, regional dynamics, engine specifications, and fuel type distributions.

---

##  Key Highlights & Metrics
* **Financial Performance:** Tracks total sales volume , gross revenue , and pricing aggregates .
* **Regional Insights:** Analyzes performance across Europe, Asia, Middle East, North America, South America, and Africa.
* **Fuel & Engine Breakdown:** Evaluates market share and max mileage for Electric, Hybrid, Petrol, and Diesel vehicles.
* **Interactive Filtering:** Allows multi-year dynamic slicers (2022–2024) and model-level drill-downs.

---

 ###  Advanced UI/UX Features: Dynamic Slicer 
* **Interactive Navigation:** Implemented a custom collapsible Slicer Panel (`X` toggle) to optimize canvas screen space.
* **Multi-Dimensional Drilling:** Enabled dynamic slicing across Regions, Transmission Types, and Vehicle Classifications, updating KPIs in real-time.
